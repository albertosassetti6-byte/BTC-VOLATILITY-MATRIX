Options Academy: What is a Bitcoin Straddle?

A Straddle is a neutral options trading strategy that involves purchasing (or selling) two options of different types—a Call option and a Put option—on the same underlying asset (e.g., Bitcoin), featuring the exact same Strike Price and the same Expiration Date.

1. Long Straddle (Buying Volatility)

Goal: Profit from large price swings in either direction.

You simultaneously buy 1 Call and 1 Put option at the current spot price. This strategy pays off when Bitcoin experiences massive volatility (e.g., macroeconomic announcements, regulatory approvals, or sudden technical breakouts). You make money if BTC moves far enough above the upper breakeven or below the lower breakeven to cover the total premium paid.

  • Max Profit: Unlimited (Upwards) / Substantial (Downwards to $0).
  • Max Loss: Capped at total premium paid ($Call + $Put).

2. Short Straddle (Selling Volatility)

Goal: Profit from market stagnation and time decay.

You simultaneously sell 1 Call and 1 Put option at the current spot price. This strategy pays off when Bitcoin remains range-bound and calm. You collect the upfront premium from buyers, hoping that options expire worthless due to low volatility or time decay (Theta).

  • Max Profit: Limited to the net premium collected.
  • Max Loss: Theoretically unlimited if BTC explodes upward or crashes.
